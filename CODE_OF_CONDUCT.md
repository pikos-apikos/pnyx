# Pnyx Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Pnyx exists to help people reason and decide together without surrendering dignity, freedom, or responsibility. We therefore commit to a community in which people can contribute regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Disagreement is expected. Dehumanization is not.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that strengthens the community include:

- treating people, evidence, dissent, and uncertainty with respect;
- criticizing ideas, code, and decisions without attacking people;
- making conflicts of interest and limitations visible;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting the public record;
- giving credit and preserving provenance;
- protecting privacy, safety, and the ability of less powerful participants to speak;
- accepting that strong negative findings may improve the final public outcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- fabricated evidence, impersonation, manipulated attribution, or deliberate provenance laundering;
- coordinated capture of discussions, reviews, votes, or contributor processes;
- using project access or moderation powers for retaliation or private advantage;
- conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or degrading.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication channels, events, and when a person represents the Pnyx project publicly.

## Reporting

Do not file a public issue about a conduct incident.

Report it privately to the repository owner or another listed maintainer using the private contact information available through their GitHub profile. Include relevant links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. If the report concerns a maintainer, contact a different maintainer when one is available.

Reports will be handled with discretion. Absolute confidentiality cannot be promised when investigation, legal obligations, or immediate safety require limited disclosure.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take proportionate action, including clarification, content removal, a private warning, temporary restriction, removal of project privileges, or a permanent ban. Decisions should be documented privately, avoid conflicts of interest, and protect both reporters and the integrity of the process.

No maintainer may adjudicate a report in which they are the accused person or have a material conflict of interest.
