Raise a Public Issue

Describe what is happening in ordinary language. Pnyx can help the issue become clearer before wider deliberation begins.

You do not need a finished solution. Begin with the problem, who or what it affects, and why it deserves public attention.
Use a short description, not a campaign slogan.
This becomes the public problem statement. Questions and evidence can be added later.
The solution remains distinct from the problem and may be challenged or replaced.
What becomes public Your title, problem statement, and optional response enter the public record. Validation, revisions, disagreement, and later outcomes remain traceable rather than silently overwritten.
